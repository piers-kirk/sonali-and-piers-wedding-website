WHAT TO WEAR

OUTFIT IDEAS FOR EACH CELEBRATION

DRESS THE PART

Outfit inspiration

Never worn Indian clothes before? No problem. Indian and Western outfits are both very welcome at every event. Here are some ideas for each celebration, and a quick guide to what everything is called.

MehendiThe WeddingThe Reception

SATURDAY 26 JUNE · EVENING

Mehendi

Colourful, relaxed and festive. This is the night for bright colours, comfortable shoes and outfits you can dance in.

Colour palette coming soon

FOR HER

Lehenga

A flared skirt with a fitted blouse and a long scarf (dupatta). The classic party outfit.

Anarkali or sharara

A long, floaty kurta dress, or a kurta with wide flared trousers. Easy to move in.

Western dress

Prefer Western? A colourful midi dress or co-ord set is perfect.

FOR HIM

Kurta

A long tunic shirt with slim trousers. Bright or pastel colours work well.

Kurta with chinos

Keep it relaxed: a kurta over light chinos or jeans.

Linen shirt

A bright linen shirt and light trousers if you’d rather go Western.

Good to knowHenna takes a while to dry, so short or easy-to-push-up sleeves help if you’d like a design.More ideas on Pinterest →

SUNDAY 27 JUNE · AFTERNOON

The Wedding

Our Hindu ceremony is the most traditional part of the weekend. Dress up, and choose light fabrics for the afternoon warmth.

Colour palette coming soon

FOR HER

Saree

A long piece of fabric draped around the body. Ask an Indian friend to help with the draping, or choose a ready-to-wear one.

Lehenga

Lighter colours and some sparkle feel lovely for an afternoon ceremony.

Formal dress

For Western outfits, a long or midi formal dress in a soft or jewel colour.

FOR HIM

Sherwani

A long, embroidered coat worn over slim trousers. The most formal option.

Kurta and Nehru jacket

A kurta with a short, collarless waistcoat on top. Smart and comfortable.

Light suit

For Western outfits, a light-coloured suit, with or without a tie.

Good to knowTraditionally, guests avoid wearing all white or bridal red. Any other colour is welcome.More ideas on Pinterest →

MONDAY 28 JUNE · EVENING

The Reception

The glamorous finale, held outdoors at night. Think evening wear, Indian or Western, and bring a layer for later.

Colour palette coming soon

FOR HER

Evening gown

A long dress in a rich colour, with some shine.

Indo-western

A modern mix, such as a cape or jacket over a lehenga or a gown.

Party saree

A sequinned or silk saree looks beautiful at night.

FOR HIM

Dinner suit

A tuxedo or dark suit for a classic look.

Bandhgala

A smart high-collared jacket, a bit like a suit with an Indian twist.

Light evening suit

A cream or pale suit with an open collar works for a summer night.

Good to knowIt’s outdoors and on grass, so block heels or flats are easier than stilettos, and evenings can feel cool.More ideas on Pinterest →

A QUICK GLOSSARY

What’s what

Lehenga

A long, flared skirt worn with a fitted blouse (choli) and a scarf (dupatta).

Saree

A single long piece of fabric draped around the body over a short blouse.

Anarkali

A long, flowing kurta dress that flares from the chest.

Sharara

Wide, flared trousers worn with a short kurta.

Dupatta

A long scarf, draped over one shoulder or both.

Kurta

A long tunic shirt, worn by men and women.

Sherwani

A long, often embroidered coat for men, buttoned to the neck.

Nehru jacket / bandhgala

A collarless waistcoat, or a high-collared jacket, worn over a kurta or shirt.

FINDING AN OUTFIT

Where to get one

Borrow

Ask Indian friends and family. Most people are delighted to lend an outfit, and to help you put it on.

Buy or rent online

Plenty of shops sell and rent Indian outfits online. Order early and allow time for alterations.

Have one made in Mauritius

Tailors and fabric shops in Port Louis can make an outfit, if you arrive a few days early.
