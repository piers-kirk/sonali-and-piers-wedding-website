MAKE A HOLIDAY OF IT

Our favourite places

Mauritius is small, but there’s a lot to see. Tap an area to see our favourite spots, and tap any name to open it in Google Maps. A driver for the day is the easiest way to get around, and most of these are within about an hour of Flic en Flac.

Just keep 26–28 June free for us!

SONALI’S SHORT LIST

If you only have a few days

Cleanest public beaches

  • Péreybère beach
  • Flic en Flac beach
  • Mont Choisy
  • Blue Bay
  • Le Morne
  • Belle Mare

Touristy sites

  • Black River Gorges National Park
  • Pamplemousses Botanical Garden
  • Île aux Cerfs
  • Chamarel Seven Coloured Earth
  • L’Aventure du Sucre
  • La Vanille Nature Park
  • Casela

Cultural spots

  • Port Louis: Aapravasi Ghat, the Central Market and Caudan Waterfront
  • Ganga Talao (Grand Bassin): our version of the Ganges
  • Le Morne, a UNESCO World Heritage site

Rum tastings & restaurants with a view

  • Rhumerie de Chamarel
  • Saint Aubin plantation
  • Bois Chéri Tea Plantation
  • Le Chamarel Restaurant, for the views
  • Château de Labourdonnais restaurant

If you do the public beaches and then some of the cultural sites, you’re honestly doing most of the island.

IF YOU HAVE MORE TIME

More things to do

IN THE WATER

Snorkelling & diving

Blue Bay, Péreybère and the reefs off the north coast are the easiest places to start.

Kitesurfing at Le Morne

One of the best kitesurf lagoons in the world, with schools for beginners.

Paddleboarding & kayaking

Many hotels lend boards and kayaks for free.

ON LAND

Le Pouce

A shorter hike with a view right over Port Louis.

Tamarind Falls

A chain of waterfalls and pools. Go with a guide, as the trails are poorly marked.

A flight over the island

Helicopter trips show off the “underwater waterfall” illusion off Le Morne.
