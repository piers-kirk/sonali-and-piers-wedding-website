DAYS OUT & THINGS TO DO

MAKE A HOLIDAY OF IT

Nine easy days out

Mauritius is small, but there’s a lot to see. We’ve split our favourites into relaxed days of two to four stops, so there’s always time for a long lunch and a swim. A driver for the day is the easiest way to get around, and most of these are within about an hour of Flic en Flac.

Just keep 26–28 June free for us!

DAY OUT 1 · THE WEST

Close to home

an easy first day after the flight

  1. 1
    Flic en Flac beach

    A long beach with a calm lagoon, and street-food stalls selling noodles and rotis. Map ↗

  2. 2
    Casela Nature Park

    Ziplines, quad safaris and walks, just inland from Flic en Flac. Map ↗

  3. 3
    Tamarin Bay

    Kayak past the mangroves, or head out early by boat to look for dolphins. Map ↗

  4. 4
    Sunset in Flic en Flac

    Back on the beach in time for the sun going down over the sea. Map ↗

Good to know Dolphin trips leave early in the morning. Choose an operator that keeps its distance from the dolphins.

DAY OUT 2 · THE SOUTH-WEST

Lakes, gorges & coloured earth

the green heart of the island

  1. 1
    Grand Bassin

    A sacred crater lake with temples and a 33 m statue of Shiva. It’s an active place of worship, so dress modestly. Map ↗

  2. 2
    Black River Gorges

    The largest national park, with rainforest viewpoints over Alexandra Falls. Map ↗

  3. 3
    Chamarel

    The Seven Coloured Earths and the Chamarel waterfall, about 100 m high. Map ↗

  4. 4
    Lunch with a view

    Le Chamarel Restaurant looks out to Le Morne, or try L’Alchimiste at the Rhumerie de Chamarel, with a rum tasting. Map ↗

Good to know The Ebony Forest in Chamarel offers guided walks if you want to stay longer.

DAY OUT 3 · THE SOUTH-WEST COAST

Le Morne & the wild south

cliffs, waves and the best views

  1. 1
    Le Morne Brabant

    Hike this UNESCO-listed mountain with a guide, about 4 hours there and back, or relax on Le Morne beach. Map ↗

  2. 2
    Maconde viewpoint

    A clifftop lookout over the coast road at Baie du Cap. Map ↗

  3. 3
    Gris Gris

    Dramatic cliffs where the waves crash in. Admire it from the top; it’s not for swimming. Map ↗

Good to know Start the Le Morne hike early, before the heat, with good shoes and plenty of water.

DAY OUT 4 · THE SOUTH

Tea, vanilla & rum

a slow day of tastings

  1. 1
    Bois Chéri

    The island’s oldest tea plantation, with a factory tour and a tasting. Closed on Sundays. Map ↗

  2. 2
    Saint Aubin

    A colonial house with a vanilla house, rum distillery and spice garden. A lovely spot for lunch. Map ↗

  3. 3
    La Vanille Nature Park

    Giant tortoises and crocodiles in a tropical forest. Map ↗

  4. 4
    Trou aux Cerfs

    A dormant volcano crater in Curepipe, a quick stop on the way back. Map ↗

Good to know Ask your driver for the Tea Route, which links these old estates.

DAY OUT 5 · THE CAPITAL

Port Louis

markets, history & street food

  1. 1
    Central Market

    Fruit, spices and souvenirs. Go in the morning and haggle, then grab a dholl puri. Map ↗

  2. 2
    Aapravasi Ghat

    The immigration depot where the indentured labour diaspora began. A UNESCO World Heritage site. Map ↗

  3. 3
    China Town

    Boulettes and dim sum, a short walk from the market. Map ↗

  4. 4
    Caudan Waterfront

    Harbourside shops and cafés, the Blue Penny Museum and the umbrella walkway. Map ↗

Good to know Horse racing at the Champ de Mars runs at weekends from noon during the season, which includes June.

DAY OUT 6 · THE NORTH

Gardens, sugar & the far north

a little bit of everything

  1. 1
    Pamplemousses Botanical Garden

    Giant water lilies and giant tortoises. Allow about an hour. Map ↗

  2. 2
    L’Aventure du Sucre

    The story of sugar in an old factory, with rum and sugar tastings. Map ↗

  3. 3
    Château de Labourdonnais

    A restored mansion and orchards. Book lunch at La Table du Château. Map ↗

  4. 4
    Cap Malheureux

    The famous Red Church, with Coin de Mire (Gunner’s Quoin) on the horizon. Map ↗

Good to know Book the château restaurant ahead, especially at weekends.

DAY OUT 7 · THE NORTH-WEST

Beach day up north

swims, cafés & an evening out

  1. 1
    Trou aux Biches & Mont Choisy

    Two of the island’s best beaches for an easy swim. Map ↗

  2. 2
    Under the sea

    Blue Safari at Trou aux Biches runs a real submarine and underwater scooters, no diving needed. Map ↗

  3. 3
    Péreybère

    A small, pretty beach with snorkelling right off the sand. Map ↗

  4. 4
    Grand Baie

    Cafés, shops and the liveliest evenings on the island. Map ↗

Good to know Book the submarine ahead. It suits children from about 4.

DAY OUT 8 · THE EAST

Île aux Cerfs

a day on the water

  1. 1
    Catamaran to Île aux Cerfs

    Sail out from the east coast with snorkelling stops and a barbecue lunch on board. Map ↗

  2. 2
    Belle Mare

    A long, quiet beach for a swim on the way back. Map ↗

Good to know Boat trips vary a lot, so check what’s included before booking.

DAY OUT 9 · THE SOUTH-EAST

Blue Bay & Mahébourg

perfect for your last day, right by the airport

  1. 1
    Mahébourg market

    A local market for produce, crafts and spices, at its best on a Monday. Map ↗

  2. 2
    Blue Bay Marine Park

    Coral and bright fish. Snorkel or take a glass-bottom boat. Map ↗

  3. 3
    Île aux Aigrettes

    A nature reserve island with giant tortoises and rare pink pigeons. Visits are guided. Map ↗

Good to know Blue Bay is the closest beach to the airport, so it’s ideal before an evening flight.

IF YOU HAVE MORE TIME

More things to do

IN THE WATER

Snorkelling & diving

Blue Bay, Péreybère and the reefs off the north coast are the easiest places to start.

Kitesurfing at Le Morne

One of the best kitesurf lagoons in the world, with schools for beginners.

Paddleboarding & kayaking

Many hotels lend boards and kayaks for free.

ON LAND

Le Pouce

A shorter hike with a view right over Port Louis.

Tamarind Falls

A chain of waterfalls and pools. Go with a guide, as the trails are poorly marked.

A flight over the island

Helicopter trips show off the “underwater waterfall” illusion off Le Morne.

CULTURE & EVENINGS

A séga evening

Mauritius’ own music and dance. Many hotels host a show once a week.

Maison Eureka

A 180-year-old plantation house in Moka with a veranda restaurant.

A spa afternoon

After a long weekend of dancing, you’ll have earned it.
