GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get from the airport?

The airport (MRU) is about an hour’s drive from Flic en Flac. Taxis and pre-booked transfers are easy to arrange. Group transfer details are coming soon.

Do I need a visa or any forms?

It depends on your passport. Many nationalities can enter visa-free for tourism, but please check the official Mauritius Passport and Immigration Office website for yours. Every traveller must also complete the free All-in-One Travel Digital Form on the official SafeMauritius site before flying (currently at least 72 hours before arrival) and save the QR code to show on arrival.

What about money and tipping?

The local currency is the Mauritian rupee (MUR). Cards are widely accepted at hotels and restaurants, but carry some cash for markets and taxis. Tipping is appreciated but not expected.

Can I drive?

Cars drive on the left in Mauritius. Many visitors hire a driver for a day of sightseeing.

Can I make a holiday of it?

Absolutely. We’ll share our favourite day trips and places to visit soon.

What’s the weather like in June?

June is winter in Mauritius: sunny days in the mid-20s °C, cooler evenings and the odd shower. The sea is still lovely for swimming.

What should I pack?

Light layers, something warmer for the evenings, swimwear, a sun hat, sunscreen, insect repellent and comfortable shoes for sightseeing.

Plugs, power & phones?

Sockets are Type C and G (230V), so UK plugs work. Mauritius is on GMT+4. Check your roaming charges, or pick up a local SIM or eSIM.

What should I wear?

Each celebration has its own suggested dress code and colours above. Indian and Western attire are both welcome.

Where can I get Indian outfits?

Borrow from friends and family, buy online before you travel, or look to the tailors and shops in Mauritius (Port Louis is a good place to start). Details coming soon.
