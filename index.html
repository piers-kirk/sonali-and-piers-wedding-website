What to expect

The Mehendi is a relaxed, colourful party the evening before the wedding. Henna (mehendi) artists paint intricate designs on the bride’s hands and feet, and guests are very welcome to have a design too. It takes around 20 minutes to dry, so it’s the perfect excuse to sit back with a drink. Expect music, dancing and plenty of food. There are no formalities, just come and enjoy yourself.

What to wear

Traditional Indian attire encouraged; Western festive attire is also welcome.

What to expect

Our wedding is a traditional Hindu ceremony, led by a priest under a decorated canopy called a mandap. It’s more relaxed than many Western ceremonies: it’s fine to chat quietly and take photos. Look out for the jaimala, when we exchange flower garlands, and the pheras, when we walk around a sacred fire seven times, making a promise with each round. The ceremony ends with the sindoor and mangalsutra, which mark the bride as married. Please arrive a little early to find your seat.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

FAQ

How do I get from the airport?

The airport (MRU) is about an hour’s drive from Flic en Flac. Taxis and pre-booked transfers are easy to arrange. Group transfer details are coming soon.

Do I need a visa or any forms?

It depends on your passport. Many nationalities can enter visa-free for tourism, but please check the official Mauritius Passport and Immigration Office website for yours. Every traveller must also complete the free All-in-One Travel Digital Form on the official SafeMauritius site before flying (currently at least 72 hours before arrival) and save the QR code to show on arrival.

What about money and tipping?

The local currency is the Mauritian rupee (MUR). Cards are widely accepted at hotels and restaurants, but carry some cash for markets and taxis. Tipping is appreciated but not expected.

Can I drive?

Cars drive on the left in Mauritius. Many visitors hire a driver for a day of sightseeing.

Can I make a holiday of it?

Absolutely. Have a look at our Explore the Island page for our favourite day trips.

What’s the weather like in June?

June is winter in Mauritius: sunny days in the mid-20s °C, cooler evenings and the odd shower. The sea is still lovely for swimming.

What should I pack?

Light layers, something warmer for the evenings, swimwear, a sun hat, sunscreen, insect repellent and comfortable shoes for sightseeing.

Plugs, power & phones?

Sockets are Type C and G (230V), so UK plugs work. Mauritius is on GMT+4. Check your roaming charges, or pick up a local SIM or eSIM.

What should I wear?

Each celebration has its own dress code on the Our Celebrations page, and our What to Wear page has outfit ideas and a quick guide to Indian clothes. Indian and Western attire are both welcome.

Where can I get Indian outfits?

Borrow from friends and family, buy online before you travel, or look to the tailors and shops in Mauritius (Port Louis is a good place to start). See our What to Wear page for more.
